# LandsandHousing

**Real estate advisory in Uyo, Akwa Ibom — with an assistant that answers honestly.**

Most property sites lose people in the first thirty seconds. A visitor asks what
you have, gets a contact form, and leaves. Or they ask a question, get something
confident but wrong, and never come back.

LandsandHousing has a different front door. Every visitor can open an advisor
chat, ask a real question, and get a real answer — grounded in our actual
listings and our actual written guidance, or an honest "we don't have that yet."

---

## What the assistant does for visitors

**It answers from the real catalogue.** Every property it shows — title, estate,
price, bedrooms, size — is a live record. It does not invent listings, and it
does not describe a property we have not verified.

**It knows the difference between buying and renting.** If someone asks for our
cheapest property, it will not answer with a rental advertised per year. Prices
are compared like with like, and the type of property you asked for is the type
you get shown.

**It won't claim towns we don't cover.** We are Uyo-based. Ask about Ikot Ekpene
or Calabar and it says we don't have listings there yet — and offers an advisor —
instead of quietly showing you something in a different location.

**It admits what it doesn't know.** Ask about agent fees, loan availability, or
whether a C of O is guaranteed, and it says plainly: *"I don't have that written
down yet, so I don't want to guess."* Then it puts you in front of a person. A
polite refusal is worth more than a confident invention.

**It treats you like a person.** One question at a time. It remembers what you
already told it, so you never repeat yourself — tell it the area up front and it
carries that through every follow-up question. It doesn't repeat the same button
bar at you, and it won't nag.

## What this does for the business

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fewer wasted enquiries** | Visitors are qualified before they reach a human. By the time an advisor follows up, they know whether the visitor is buying or renting, in which area, at what budget, for how many bedrooms, and on what timeline. |
| **Enquiries that arrive complete** | Every conversation captures name, phone and email, in whatever order the visitor happens to give them — including mid-sentence. |
| **Inspections actually booked** | A visitor can book an inspection from inside the conversation, choosing the property and a date. |
| **A qualified summary, not a raw transcript** | The team receives a short structured brief: purpose, type, preferred area, bedrooms, budget, property of interest, requested date. |
| **Nothing falls through the gap** | Every booking request is emailed to the leads inbox the moment it is made. |
| **Fewer awkward conversations** | Visitors who were going to be frustrated by a wrong answer now get a straight answer, or a straight referral. |

## What a visitor sees

1. Lands on the page and browses as they normally would.
2. Taps **Speak With an Advisor** — the chat opens, centred, with Aisha.
3. Answers one question at a time, or types a request in their own words.
   *"I need a house at Shelter"* and *"I.need.a.propertyat.ewet.housing"* both
   work; nobody has to fill in a form correctly.
4. Sees matching properties as cards, with the option to book an inspection.
5. Receives their inspection request by email, and the team receives the brief.

## Coverage and honesty rules

These are not settings. They are rules the assistant is held to, and they were
each added because a plausible wrong answer is worse than no answer:

- Never present a listing from an area we do not cover as though we do.
- Never mix a rental with a sale price when comparing.
- Never answer a factual question about fees, finance, or legal guarantees from
  anything other than our own written guidance.
- Never repeat a question more than twice before offering a person.
- Never lose a name, number, or budget the visitor already gave.
